## Earlier directions

Quiet Ledger Desk, Editorial Memory Workspace, Precision Console, and the later warm-minimalism variations are options to discuss, not a required screen composition. Compare the same real task across directions before choosing. Include long content, mobile width, keyboard use, loading, empty, and failure states.

## Source provenance

The previous audit dated July 2026 cited the following sources. This list preserves provenance; it is not a fresh usability study or endorsement of every old interpretation.

- [Vercel interface guidelines](https://vercel.com/design/guidelines) and [Geist](https://vercel.com/geist/introduction): interaction and layout consistency.
- [Linear method](https://linear.app/method/introduction), [design process](https://linear.app/method/manage-design-projects), and [quality](https://linear.app/quality): focused workflows and narrowing alternatives.
- [OpenAI brand](https://openai.com/brand/) and [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/): hierarchy and conversational surfaces.
- [Notion](https://www.notion.com/product) and [Calendar](https://www.notion.com/product/calendar): editable knowledge and time context.
- [Superhuman](https://superhuman.com/products/mail): triage and follow-up.
- [Obsidian graph](https://obsidian.md/help/plugins/graph): inspectable relationships.
- [NN/g heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/) and [minimalist design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/aesthetic-minimalist-design/): status, recovery, and information restraint.
- [WIRED on Cursor](https://www.wired.com/story/cursor-launches-pro-design-tools-figma): secondary reporting on design tooling, not an Atlas requirement.
